Data Quality Methodology

Quality ratings are based on how far average vaccination coverage deviates from the 100% target. Technical auditors can sort and filter this data to identify reporting gaps and ensure internal consistency.

  • ✅ Excellent: <2% deviation from target
  • ☑️ Good: 2–5% deviation
  • ⚠️ Fair: 5–10% deviation
  • ❌ Poor: >10% deviation — requires investigation
