Research Finding: Funding & Outbreak Correlation

Analysis shows a strong inverse correlation between economic development and vaccine-preventable disease outbreaks. Countries in lower economic tiers consistently show higher infection rates despite improving vaccination coverage. This split-screen comparison allows researchers to identify disparities and advocate for targeted funding increases.
