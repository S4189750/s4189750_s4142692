Mission & Target Audience

The Global Vaccine Impact Database was created to address a critical gap in accessible, trustworthy global health information. In an era of information overload and conflicting narratives, we provide an empirical source of truth for vaccination milestones spanning 194 countries.

We serve diverse users—from concerned citizens seeking basic macro metrics to researchers conducting thorough epidemiological analysis—by implementing structural relational schema designs and intuitive analytical query portals.

Structural Transparency

All database records map cleanly across regional, temporal, and economic dimensions without convoluted processing pipelines.

Unbiased Reporting

We present raw global data configurations derived directly from audited records, helping users bypass editorialized summaries.

Public Health Impact

Our ultimate goal is to drive informed decision-making and improve public health outcomes through data-driven insights.

How to use this website

Level 1: Overview

Start with the Landing page and the mission statement to understand the purpose of the platform.

Level 2: Explore

Filter vaccination rates by country or region and examine complex infection data grouped by national economic status.

Level 3: Analysis

Deep-dive into multi-year vaccination improvements and identify specific countries with above-average infection rates.
