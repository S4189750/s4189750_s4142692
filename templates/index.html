Mission Overview

The Global Vaccine Impact Database provides transparent, unbiased access to vaccination coverage data across 194 countries from 2000 to 2024. Our mission is to serve as the definitive single source of truth for researchers, policymakers, and health professionals seeking to understand global immunization trends and their impact on public health.

By integrating vaccination records, infection data, economic indicators, and demographic information, we enable evidence-based analysis of vaccine efficacy, coverage gaps, and the relationship between healthcare access and disease prevention.
